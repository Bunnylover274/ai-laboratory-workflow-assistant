FICTIONAL TRAINING SOP — Rapid Salmonella Screening

Purpose
Screen poultry rinsate samples for Salmonella using a fictional PCR method.
This document is for software training only and must not be used in a laboratory.

Procedure
1. A Sample Receiving Technician verifies the sample ID, sample type, collection
   date, receipt date, and container condition. Reject leaking or unlabeled samples.
2. A Laboratory Technician transfers 25 mL of poultry rinsate to an enrichment
   vessel and adds 225 mL of enrichment broth.
3. Incubate the enrichment at 37 C for 18 to 24 hours. Record the incubator ID,
   start time, end time, and actual temperature.
4. Transfer an aliquot to the PCR preparation area. Record the extraction batch
   ID and PCR plate ID.
5. Run the fictional PCR assay with one positive control and one negative control.
6. The run is valid only when the positive control is detected and the negative
   control is not detected. A failed control requires review and repeat testing.
7. A qualified reviewer records the final sample result as Detected, Not Detected,
   or Inconclusive and approves the result before release.

The SOP does not define the instrument model, PCR target, result threshold,
repeat-testing limit, or sample-retention period.
